Why this and not a website

It works with no connection

Everything computes on your own machine. No account, no upload, no server that can be down at 2am the night before it is due, and nothing that stops working when the campus wifi does. This was a deliberate design decision, not an accident of scope.

It shows the work

An answer with no derivation is worth very little when the marks are for method. Solve prints its steps, integration names whether it was exact or numerical, pKa shows the substituent correction it applied, and engineering results report the unit conversions they performed.

Free, and there is no student tier

MIT licensed and open source. No trial, no seat, no expiry, no academic-verification email. You keep it after you graduate, which is not true of most of what your department licenses.

It tells you when it doesn't know

An unconjugated molecule reports “no λmax” rather than inventing one. Predicted spectra are labelled predictions. Where a measured value would need a citation the tool does not have, it says the value is absent and why, instead of filling it in. Learning from a tool that quietly guesses is worse than not having one.

What it does not do — worth knowing before you install.

Citations are Bluebook legal only. There is no APA, MLA, Chicago, IEEE or ACS style yet, so for a lab report or an essay bibliography you still need your reference manager. The citation machinery here was built for patent and legal drafting. Predicted spectra are estimates from published additivity rules — use them to assign peaks you already have, never as a substitute for running the spectrum. And it does analysis and reporting, not general programming: a Simulink model still needs MATLAB.

Get started

Two minutes: unzip, run the installer, fully restart Word (all windows — this is the step people miss), then Insert → Add-ins → Developer Add-ins → JurisLab. It installs per-user and needs no admin rights, though a locked-down university machine may still block add-in installation — worth trying on a personal laptop first.
